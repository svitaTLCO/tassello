[perche.titolo]
A Swiss Army knife, not a suite

[perche.intro]
Tassello puts the ability to work on your own files in everyone’s hands, as simply as possible. It does the basic, everyday work, and does it tidily. It is free for everyone, including businesses and schools.

[perche.necessario_titolo]
Just what you need

[perche.necessario]
The features you actually need for documents, spreadsheets, pages to print or publish, photos and graphics, audio and video, chosen one by one and kept in order.

[perche.leggero_titolo]
Lightweight

[perche.leggero]
No suite to install and no account: a file of about one megabyte that opens in your browser, even offline. If you prefer, you can install it as an app.

[perche.tuoi_titolo]
Your files stay yours

[perche.tuoi]
Everything happens in your browser: no server receives your files, not even your recordings.

[perche.accessibile_titolo]
Accessible by principle

[perche.accessibile]
It works with a screen reader and with the keyboard alone. Without that, no tassello is released.

[perche.non_e]
<b>What it is not.</b> Tassello does not replace professional tools: if your work needs macros, tracked changes, professional publishing layout, professional photo retouching, professional video editing or virtual instruments and MIDI, keep using the right program. Nor is it a clone of famous apps: it opens and saves their formats so you can exchange files with anyone, but it has its own design, with features chosen for everyday work and all reachable from the keyboard.

[accessibilita.titolo]
Accessibility: the requirement, not an extra

[accessibilita.intro]
A tassello is released only if it works with a screen reader and with the keyboard alone. It is not an option to switch on: it is how every command, dialog and message is designed.

[accessibilita.tastiera_titolo]
Keyboard

[accessibilita.tastiera]
<kbd>F6</kbd> moves between areas, <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Q</kbd> finds any command, <kbd>F1</kbd> lists the shortcuts.

[accessibilita.lettore_titolo]
Screen readers

[accessibilita.lettore]
Standard ARIA roles and announcements for cells, states and messages, designed for NVDA, JAWS and Narrator.

[accessibilita.lettura_titolo]
Reading

[accessibilita.lettura]
Reading mode, high contrast, dark theme, read aloud and an accessibility checker for documents and pages; in Impagina the reading order of frames is explicit and <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>I</kbd> describes the page.

[accessibilita.ascolto_titolo]
Listening

[accessibilita.ascolto]
In Audio the waveform plays back the point where you are, and you can jump from one pause in the speech to the next; in Video the checker reviews captions, contrast and flashing; in Immagini, <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>I</kbd> describes the photo, its light and the adjustments you changed, and selections, masks and cut-outs can be described in words; in Disegno, path nodes are a list and a cursor draws from the keyboard.

[accessibilita.segnalazioni]
Do you use NVDA, JAWS or Narrator? Your <a href="https://github.com/svitaTLCO/tassello/issues/new?template=accessibilita.yml">reports</a> are very welcome: an accessibility problem is a bug to fix, not an improvement to consider.

[privacy.titolo]
Privacy

[privacy.testo]
      <p>Your documents stay in your browser and on your computer. The only feature that uses an external service is voice dictation in Tassello Ufficio: in Chrome the audio is sent to Google’s servers, in Edge to Microsoft’s. If you do not use it, nothing leaves your device.</p>
      <p>Impagina reads the folder you link only when you ask it to and never changes it; it downloads nothing, not even the images listed in a feed, and it creates QR codes offline.</p>
      <p>Tassello Media uses the microphone only when you ask it to, and your recordings, files and exported videos stay on your computer.</p>
      <p>Tassello Grafica never changes your original photos, and exported files contain neither camera data nor the place where the photo was taken. Background removal also happens entirely on your computer, with no outside service.</p>
      <p>This applies to the official copy. A copy modified by someone else may behave differently: one more reason to <a href="#sicurezza">download Tassello only from here</a>.</p>

[sicurezza.titolo]
Security: use only the official copy

[sicurezza.intro]
Tassello is free software: anyone can study and modify it. That is why altered copies may circulate, even under the same name, that read your documents or ask you for personal data. Here is how to recognise the official one.

[sicurezza.scarica_titolo]
Download only from here

[sicurezza.scarica]
The only official addresses are <b>svitatlco.github.io/tassello</b> and the <a href="https://github.com/svitaTLCO/tassello/releases">GitHub releases</a>. A file received by email, on a USB stick or from other websites may have been modified: do not open it, download it again from here. To recommend Tassello, share the link instead of the file.

[sicurezza.mai_titolo]
What it never does

[sicurezza.mai]
The official copy never asks for passwords, accounts or payments, never offers to download updates or other programs and never connects to the internet: a security rule written into the file forbids it. The only exception is dictation in Tassello Ufficio, which uses the browser’s service.

[sicurezza.verifica_titolo]
Verify a copy

[sicurezza.verifica]
On the <a href="verifica.html">Verify a copy</a> page, choose the file: the website tells you whether it is identical to an official version. The file never leaves your computer.

[sicurezza.app]

The app always comes from this website, works offline and updates itself.

[verifica.come_titolo]
How it works

[verifica.come]
    <p>For every published version we record the file’s <b>SHA-256 fingerprint</b>: a sequence of 64 characters that changes completely if even a single letter of the file changes. This page computes your file’s fingerprint and looks it up in the lists of official versions of <a href="{{BASE}}versioni.json">Tassello Ufficio</a>, <a href="{{BASE}}versioni-media.json">Tassello Media</a> and <a href="{{BASE}}versioni-grafica.json">Tassello Grafica</a>.</p>
    <p>If the file is not recognised as official, someone may have modified it, or a program may have saved it again (for example with the browser’s “Save page as”). Either way, do not use it for important documents and <a href="./">download the official copy</a>.</p>
