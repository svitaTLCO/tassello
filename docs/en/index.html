Free · Open source · Accessible

The essentials for working on files.

Tassello is a family of small free tools for everyday productivity: writing, filling in forms, doing the numbers, laying out pages, improving photos and drawing, recording and editing audio and video. Each one is a single HTML file that opens in your browser, with no bulky suite to install and no account. A Swiss Army knife: tidy and lightweight.

Tassello Ufficio: Testo, Calcolo and Impagina, for documents, spreadsheets and page layout Tassello Media: Audio and Video, for recording and editing Tassello Grafica: Immagini and Disegno, for photos and graphics

Works in recent Chrome and Edge, even offline. The programs themselves are in Italian for now.

A Swiss Army knife, not a suite

Tassello puts the ability to work on your own files in everyone’s hands, as simply as possible. It does the basic, everyday work, and does it tidily. It is free for everyone, including businesses and schools.

Just what you need

The features you actually need for documents, spreadsheets, pages to print or publish, photos and graphics, audio and video, chosen one by one and kept in order.

Lightweight

No suite to install and no account: a file of about one megabyte that opens in your browser, even offline. If you prefer, you can install it as an app.

Your files stay yours

Everything happens in your browser: no server receives your files, not even your recordings.

Accessible by principle

It works with a screen reader and with the keyboard alone. Without that, no tassello is released.

What it is not. Tassello does not replace professional tools: if your work needs macros, tracked changes, professional publishing layout, professional photo retouching, professional video editing or virtual instruments and MIDI, keep using the right program. Nor is it a clone of famous apps: it opens and saves their formats so you can exchange files with anyone, but it has its own design, with features chosen for everyday work and all reachable from the keyboard.

Tassello Ufficio: Testo, Calcolo and Impagina

For everyday documents, spreadsheets and pages. It opens and saves DOCX and XLSX, so you can exchange files with people who use other programs; Impagina exports PNG for social media and PDF for print. The interface is in Italian.

Testo

The word processor, for letters, reports, theses and forms.

  • Editable styles, multilevel lists, tables, images and captions
  • Sections, headers with a logo, notes, table of contents and index
  • Cross-references, citations and APA or Chicago bibliography
  • Fillable forms, equations, comments, mail merge
  • Opens and saves DOCX, exports HTML and PDF through printing

Calcolo

The spreadsheet, for budgets, lists and analysis.

  • More than a hundred functions in Italian or English, dynamic arrays
  • Pivot tables with calculated fields, filters and chart
  • Conditional formatting, data validation, defined names, notes
  • Twelve chart types, which you can also read as a table or hear as sounds
  • Opens and saves XLSX and CSV

Impagina

The layout editor, for posts and carousels, stories, flyers, posters, brochures, newsletters, slides, business cards, invitations and certificates.

  • 137 templates in 23 formats, chosen by format and purpose, plus your own saved templates
  • Margins, columns, grid and master pages with page numbers and logo; several brand kits with colours, fonts and logo
  • Text that flows across frames and pages and wraps around frames, paragraph and character styles, eye-catching text effects
  • Rotated frames with shadows and gradients; photos adjusted inside their frame, with adjustments copied from one to another; offline QR codes, tables and graphic elements
  • Data from CSV, XLSX, JSON or Calcolo to make one page per row; RSS feeds, text from DOCX and a linked folder, including Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox
  • Colour themes, image shapes, photo grids, smart guides and resizing to another format
  • Bleed and crop marks, posters and banners printed as tiles on A4 or A3 sheets, full-screen slideshow; exports PNG and PDF
  • Reading order, alternative text and an accessibility check for every page; tables stay readable in the PDF

Tassello Media: Audio and Video

For recording and cleaning up your voice, making a podcast or editing a video for school or work. Everything works from the keyboard and with a screen reader: you hear where you are, jump from one pause in the speech to the next, and every property has a field with its own name. The interface is in Italian.

Audio

For podcasts, interviews, lessons and small music productions.

  • Multiple tracks with volume, pan, mute, solo, bass, treble, compression and reverb
  • Regions to move, split and loop; tempo, metronome, cycle and recording over the other tracks
  • Normalize to peak or perceived loudness (LUFS), fades, tempo changes without altering pitch
  • Noise reduction, equalizer, compressor, shorten pauses
  • Exports the song or separate tracks as WAV, M4A and WebM; saves the project as a ZIP file

Video

For presentations, tutorials and memories to share.

  • Video track with splitting, trimming, speed, volume, filters, rotation
  • Transitions, title cards, titles and an audio track for music and voice-over
  • Captions to write, import and export as SRT and WebVTT
  • Accessibility check: captions, reading time, contrast and flashing
  • Exports MP4 or WebM, audio only or a single frame; saves the project as a ZIP file
  • Exports one frame at a time, even with the tab in the background
Download Tassello Media Try Tassello Media in your browser

A file of about 470 KB for recent Chrome and Edge, even offline. It covers the basics: very long files need a lot of memory, and videos the browser cannot decode are exported in real time instead of one frame at a time. Always keep a copy of your originals.

Tassello Grafica: Immagini and Disegno

For improving photos and making everyday graphics: a photo for social media, a portrait, a document to make readable, a flyer or an image for a post. Everything works from the keyboard and with a screen reader: the photo and its light are described in words, and every adjustment is a slider with its own name. The interface is in Italian.

Immagini

The photo editor, for photos to share, portraits, landscapes, documents and products.

  • Guided plans by goal: social media, portrait, landscape, document or receipt, product on a light background
  • Framing with rotation, straightening and ready-made formats; light, colour, curves, eight colour ranges, detail and vignetting
  • Selections (lasso, brush, magic wand, colour range) and masks with local adjustments
  • Background removal with no internet and no AI; healing, clone stamp and red-eye removal
  • Ready-made looks and looks imported from .cube LUT files, with adjustable strength
  • A histogram described in words, with a warning when highlights clip or shadows block up
  • Opens JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, BMP, HEIC photos and the preview inside RAW files; exports JPEG, PNG or WebP without camera data

Disegno

The drawing tool, for posts, stories, banners, flyers and icons.

  • Canvases in today’s formats or any size you like, with a solid or transparent background
  • A pen with node editing, pencil, pressure-sensitive brush and eraser; combine and subtract shapes and lines, cut with a line, simplify paths
  • Shapes, arrows, speech bubbles and text, even along a curve, convertible to paths, with gradients, dashes, shadows and blending
  • Images with the adjustments, masks and background removal from Immagini, and clipping inside a shape
  • Multiple selection, groups, layers, alignment, distribution and smart guides
  • Saves editable SVG with a title and description; exports PNG or JPEG
  • Accessibility check: text contrast and a description of the drawing
Download Tassello Grafica Try Tassello Grafica in your browser

A file of about 560 KB for recent Chrome and Edge, even offline. It covers the basics: Immagini works on one photo at a time, opens HEIC photos only where the system can decode HEVC and opens the camera’s preview of RAW files; background removal uses classic algorithms, so subjects touching the edges, fine hair or glass need a box or a few strokes; converting text to paths needs permission to read installed fonts, or the font file. Always keep a copy of your originals.

Accessibility: the requirement, not an extra

A tassello is released only if it works with a screen reader and with the keyboard alone. It is not an option to switch on: it is how every command, dialog and message is designed.

Keyboard

F6 moves between areas, Alt+Q finds any command, F1 lists the shortcuts.

Screen readers

Standard ARIA roles and announcements for cells, states and messages, designed for NVDA, JAWS and Narrator.

Reading

Reading mode, high contrast, dark theme, read aloud and an accessibility checker for documents and pages; in Impagina the reading order of frames is explicit and Alt+Shift+I describes the page.

Listening

In Audio the waveform plays back the point where you are, and you can jump from one pause in the speech to the next; in Video the checker reviews captions, contrast and flashing; in Immagini, Alt+Shift+I describes the photo, its light and the adjustments you changed, and selections, masks and cut-outs can be described in words; in Disegno, path nodes are a list and a cursor draws from the keyboard.

Do you use NVDA, JAWS or Narrator? Your reports are very welcome: an accessibility problem is a bug to fix, not an improvement to consider.

Privacy

Your documents stay in your browser and on your computer. The only feature that uses an external service is voice dictation in Tassello Ufficio: in Chrome the audio is sent to Google’s servers, in Edge to Microsoft’s. If you do not use it, nothing leaves your device.

Impagina reads the folder you link only when you ask it to and never changes it; it downloads nothing, not even the images listed in a feed, and it creates QR codes offline.

Tassello Media uses the microphone only when you ask it to, and your recordings, files and exported videos stay on your computer.

Tassello Grafica never changes your original photos, and exported files contain neither camera data nor the place where the photo was taken. Background removal also happens entirely on your computer, with no outside service.

This applies to the official copy. A copy modified by someone else may behave differently: one more reason to download Tassello only from here.

Security: use only the official copy

Tassello is free software: anyone can study and modify it. That is why altered copies may circulate, even under the same name, that read your documents or ask you for personal data. Here is how to recognise the official one.

Download only from here

The only official addresses are svitatlco.github.io/tassello and the GitHub releases. A file received by email, on a USB stick or from other websites may have been modified: do not open it, download it again from here. To recommend Tassello, share the link instead of the file.

What it never does

The official copy never asks for passwords, accounts or payments, never offers to download updates or other programs and never connects to the internet: a security rule written into the file forbids it. The only exception is dictation in Tassello Ufficio, which uses the browser’s service.

Verify a copy

On the Verify a copy page, choose the file: the website tells you whether it is identical to an official version. The file never leaves your computer.

Install it as an app

In Chrome or Edge, open Tassello Ufficio, Tassello Media or Tassello Grafica in the browser and choose Install in the address bar or in the menu. The app always comes from this website, works offline and updates itself.

Found a suspicious copy? Report it on GitHub.
