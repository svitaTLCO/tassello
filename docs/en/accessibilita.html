Accessibility statement

For Tassello, accessibility is the condition for releasing a tool. Here is what we have checked, where we stand and what does not work well yet, so you know what to expect.

What it covers

  • Tassello Ufficio 1.10.0: Testo, Calcolo and Impagina
  • Tassello Media 1.4.0: Audio and Video
  • Tassello Grafica 1.3.1: Immagini and Disegno
  • this website and the verification page

It applies to recent Chrome or Edge on a computer; testing was done on Windows. Tablets, phones, the Mac with VoiceOver and other browsers are not supported yet. The app interface is in Italian.

Conformance status

Tassello is partially conformant with WCAG 2.2 level AA. Our checks find no remaining failures against level A and AA criteria, apart from the known limitations below; conformance is not yet confirmed with real screen readers, so we state it as partial until the testing group has finished.

How we evaluated it

  • A WCAG 2.2 AA evaluation in Edge on Windows, with axe-core and custom checks: about 1,000 states of the seven apps and the website, in four themes and with forced colours, repeated after the fixes.
  • Automated real-browser tests for each tool, which also read the exact screen reader announcements.
  • A task-by-task analysis, app by app, of the work usually done by looking.
  • Testing with blind people, in progress, using the testing guide (in Italian).

What Tassello offers

  • Everything works from the keyboard. Descriptions use the same key in every app: Alt+Shift+I what is in front of you, D the whole, R where the selected item is, L light or levels, O the comparison with the original.
  • Visual results in words and sounds: position and overlaps of frames and objects, light and colours of a photo, scenes and speech in a video, audio levels, charts and histograms you can listen to, colours by name.
  • Accessibility checks for what you make, Impagina PDFs with headings, paragraphs and alternative text, captions in Video.
  • Dark theme, high contrast, forced colours, reduced motion, reflow down to 320 px wide.

Known limitations

  • Behaviour with NVDA, JAWS and Narrator is still to be confirmed, especially for announcements joined into one sentence and for interactive areas: the photo, the histogram, the video preview, the Calcolo grid.
  • Some Tassello Ufficio dialogs correct an out-of-range value without saying which limit was used.
  • Some visual tasks are still hard without sight: placing frames on the grid in Impagina, following the cut-out edge in Immagini, exploring a drawing with a cursor, hearing light and colour before and after in Video. The flash check does not yet detect flashes on part of the screen or red flashes.
  • Descriptions measure pixels and sound without artificial intelligence: they do not recognise people or objects.

Feedback

If something does not work with the technology you use, report it with the Accessibility problem form: we treat it as a bug and it comes first. The full statement is in the repository.
